# Sources and boundaries

This is an original synthesis for agent-assisted decisions, not a book adaptation or the work of the people named below. One agent applies selected methods. It does not summon people, recreate their personalities, or claim endorsement.

“First principles” here means making consequential premises explicit, checking what supports them, and reconstructing options. A buyer's behavior is contingent evidence, not an axiom. The five lenses are complementary commercial discovery methods; they are not all definitions of first principles and do not form one historically established theory.

| Inspiration | What this skill takes | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| Richard Feynman | Look for ways the favored explanation could mislead you; report contrary evidence. Not celebrity roleplay. | [Cargo Cult Science, Caltech, 1974](https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/51/2/CargoCult.htm) |
| Clayton Christensen and collaborators | The progress sought in a particular circumstance; investigate switching and existing alternatives. | [Christensen Institute: Jobs to Be Done](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/) |
| W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne | Reconsider which factors to eliminate, reduce, raise or create. This does not establish an uncontested market. | [Four Actions Framework](https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/tools/four-actions-framework/) |
| Rob Fitzpatrick | Ask about concrete past behavior; distinguish praise from commitment. | [The Mom Test, author site](https://www.momtestbook.com/) |
| Teresa Torres | Keep outcomes, opportunities, solutions and assumptions distinct; test assumptions that matter to a decision. | [Discovering Solutions, Product Talk](https://www.producttalk.org/discovering-solutions/) |

The comparison between rival explanations, entity-specific evidence, conditional recommendations, test stop versus market rejection, and output format are this project's operational synthesis. No primary source or famous name proves that this skill improves model performance. See the repository's published evaluations for actual observed differences and limitations.
